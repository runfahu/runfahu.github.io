Integer Formulation for the Houtman-Maks Index

This note:

  • introduces the Houtman–Maks Index in revealed preference theory and its connection to rationalizability and Afriat’s inequalities;
  • develops a geometric and combinatorial perspective based on sign vectors, hypersum spans, and convex hulls, and uses it to derive an integer programming formulation for the index;
  • compares the resulting formulation with an existing formulation and illustrates its computational performance through numerical experiments.

I presented this topic at MATH6104: Algebraic Combinatorics.
